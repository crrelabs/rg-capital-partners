The Opportunity

Why Twin Oaks?

Twin Oaks Shopping Center is a 30,704 SF multi-tenant retail center on North Avenue (Highway 58), Belton's primary retail corridor, less than two miles from I-49. The center is 100% leased on triple-net (NNN) leases with contractual annual rent increases, producing durable, largely inflation-protected cash flow from Day 1, and it sits directly in the path of more than $350 million of announced new development, including the $228M Uptown entertainment district planned on the very same corridor.

Durable, In-Place NNN Cash Flow

Acquired at an 8.8% going-in cap rate and delivering roughly 7.9% cash-on-cash in Year 1. All leases are triple-net, so tenants reimburse taxes, insurance, and CAM, insulating investors from expense inflation.

Contractual Rent Growth Built In

Every lease carries scheduled escalators (annual or biennial bumps, typically 2-3%). In-place base rent grows from $283K to $313K over the hold with no leasing risk assumed, so NOI compounds on autopilot.

Fastest-Growing Submarket in Metro KC

Belton is among Missouri's fastest-growing cities, up 11% since 2020. The $228M Uptown District (660+ homes, B&B Theatres entertainment complex) is planned on North Avenue, with a new Price Chopper-anchored center already underway on the corridor.

Recession-Resistant, E-Commerce-Proof Tenants

Anchored by NAPA Auto Parts and Iron Gates Gym, with a laundromat, barber, wireless store, and specialty retail, all service-based, internet-resistant uses that draw consistent daily traffic and renew in place.

Investment Thesis

A Stabilized Asset in a Growth Path

Unlike a heavy value-add project, Twin Oaks is bought fully stabilized and cash-flowing. The strategy is simple and low-risk: acquire well below replacement cost, collect strong NNN income from Day 1, let contractual escalators and Belton's growth lift NOI, and exit into a market where hundreds of millions of dollars of new investment are compressing cap rates.

Why This Deal Works

  • ◆ 100% occupied across the entire 30,704 SF, with no lease-up risk and no vacancy assumption needed to hit projections
  • ◆ True NNN structure, where tenants reimburse taxes, insurance, and CAM, protecting the investor from expense inflation
  • ◆ Contractual escalators in every lease grow income without releasing or re-tenanting
  • ◆ Staggered expirations from 2028 through 2031 smooth rollover exposure over the hold
  • ◆ $260K working-capital reserve funded at closing, with no anticipated capital calls

Risk Mitigants

  • ◆ ~$747,500 first-year bonus depreciation (about 64% of invested equity) provides significant tax shelter for investors
  • ◆ 1.55x DSCR in Year 1, rising to 1.60x, a comfortable debt coverage cushion
  • ◆ Day-1 positive cash flow at ~7.9% cash-on-cash, ahead of the 7% preferred return
  • ◆ Essential, service-based tenant mix (auto parts, laundry, fitness, wireless) resistant to e-commerce
  • ◆ Low basis at $94/SF provides downside protection versus replacement cost
  • ◆ Amortizing debt builds ~$201K of equity through principal paydown over the hold
  • ◆ Rovira PM manages in-house with no third-party dependency
