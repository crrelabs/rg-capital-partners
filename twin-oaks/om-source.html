The Opportunity
Investment Overview

Twin Oaks Shopping Center is a 30,704 SF multi-tenant retail center on North Avenue (Highway 58), Belton's primary retail corridor, less than two miles from I-49. The center is 100% leased on triple-net leases with contractual annual rent increases, producing durable cash flow from Day 1, and sits directly in the path of more than $350 million of announced new development, including the $228M Uptown entertainment district on the same corridor.

Durable, In-Place NNN Cash Flow

An 8.8% going-in cap rate delivering ~7.9% cash-on-cash in Year 1. Triple-net leases pass taxes, insurance and CAM to tenants, insulating investors from expense inflation.

Contractual Rent Growth Built In

Every lease carries scheduled 2-3% escalators. In-place base rent grows from $283K to $313K over the hold with no leasing risk assumed.

Fastest-Growing KC Submarket

Belton is up 11% since 2020. The $228M Uptown District (660+ homes, B&B Theatres complex) is planned on North Avenue, with a Price Chopper-anchored center already underway on the corridor.

E-Commerce-Proof Tenant Mix

Anchored by NAPA Auto Parts and Iron Gates Gym, with a laundromat, barber, wireless, and specialty retail, all service uses that drive daily traffic and renew in place.

Investment Thesis
A Stabilized Asset in a Growth Path

Unlike a heavy value-add project, Twin Oaks is acquired fully stabilized and cash-flowing. The strategy is simple and low-risk: buy well below replacement cost, collect strong NNN income from Day 1, let contractual escalators and Belton's growth lift NOI, and exit into a market where hundreds of millions of dollars of new investment are compressing cap rates.
